9) Baseline correction regression (2026-10-22, after GPT review)
Problem found: preview/post treated every nonzero account without a baseline as
eligible and posted the entire current balance. A legacy account that received a
post-migration deposit would therefore overstate customer liability.
Fix:
  - baseline gap = account balance - net CustomerLiability already in journals;
  - positive gap: debit OpeningBalanceOffset / credit CustomerLiability;
  - negative gap: debit CustomerLiability / credit OpeningBalanceOffset;
  - gap == 0 and accounts with an existing baseline are skipped;
  - account balance + journal liability are read together and posting uses a
    serializable transaction + unique baseline SourceKey;
  - preview/post/audit totals are per currency (TRY/USD/EUR/GBP); no mixed total.

Regression tests (DigitalBanking.Tests):
  LegacyBalanceThenPostMigrationDepositBaselineCoversOnlyTheLegacyGap
  NewAccountDepositIsFullyJournaledAndBaselineCreatesNothing
  BaselineCanExceedCurrentBalanceAfterPostMigrationDebit
  BaselineDecreasesLiabilityWhenJournalExceedsAccountBalance
  BaselineTotalsArePerCurrencyAndNeverMixCurrencies
  AccountingBaselineRaceTests.BaselineAndApprovalRaceLeaveExactlyOneBaselineGap
